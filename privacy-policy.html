Infinite Wallpapers – Privacy Policy

Android app · Effective date: 2026-09-27

Infinite Wallpapers finds wallpapers on the web for a theme you choose and sets them on your phone. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: there is no account, and the developer does not receive or store any of your data.

What the app does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising.
  • No crash reporting.
  • The developer runs no servers, so nothing is sent to the developer.
  • The app does not read your photos, contacts, location, or other files.

What is stored on your phone

Your settings (chosen theme, change frequency, home/lock screen choice, appearance), a short history of recent wallpapers with their web addresses, the list of images you skipped, and copies of recently downloaded wallpapers are saved in the app's private storage on your phone.

If Android backup is turned on, your settings may be included in your device's backup (for example, your Google account backup), as with most apps. The backup excludes your Serper API key and the downloaded wallpapers.

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes all of this.

What is sent over the internet

To find and download wallpapers, the app connects directly from your phone to these services. All connections use HTTPS.

  • Wallhaven (wallhaven.cc): the app sends the theme's search text (a built-in theme or text you type). Wallhaven is a free wallpaper site and needs no key.
  • Serper (google.serper.dev): only if you add your own Serper API key in Settings. The app sends the search text and your key so Serper can return Google Images results.
  • Image hosts: the chosen image is downloaded from the website where it is hosted. These can be any third-party website that appears in the results.

As with any internet request, these services can see your IP address and basic request details (such as the app's user agent). Their own privacy policies apply to what they do with it. The developer has no control over and no access to that data.

When automatic changes are on, the app does this in the background at the frequency you pick.

Your Serper API key

The key is optional. It is stored in the app's private storage on your phone, excluded from backups, and sent only to google.serper.dev with your searches. You can remove it at any time in Settings.

Content

Searches are always safe-search only (Wallhaven's SFW filter; Serper's safe search). Filters are not perfect, and images come from third-party websites, so the developer cannot guarantee every result. Use "Skip & block" to remove an image you don't want. Each image belongs to its creator.

Permissions

  • Internet: to search for and download wallpapers.
  • Set wallpaper: to change your home and lock screen wallpaper.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
