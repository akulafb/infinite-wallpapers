## Full description (max 4000)

A new wallpaper, found for you.

Pick a theme, or describe your own, like "rainy Tokyo street at night". The app finds a matching wallpaper, fits it to your screen, and sets it.

- Changes hourly, daily, weekly, or when you tap
- Home screen, lock screen, or both
- Skip ones you don't like, go back to old ones
- No account, no ads, no tracking

Images come from Wallhaven, or Google Images with your own free Serper key. Safe search is always on. Each image belongs to its creator.
